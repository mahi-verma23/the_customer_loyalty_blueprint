# The Customer Loyalty Blueprint
Customer Loyalty Blueprint is an end-to-end customer analytics project using SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze shopping behavior, loyalty trends, and revenue patterns. The project combines SQL-driven insights, customer segmentation, and interactive dashboards to support data-driven marketing, retention, and business growth strategies.

Understanding why customers return , and what drives them to spend more -is one of the biggest challenges in retail analytics.

## What are the differentiators?
- End-to-end workflow from data cleaning to dashboard reporting
- Business-focused SQL analysis instead of only descriptive charts
- Customer segmentation using behavioral metrics
- Interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI tracking
- Focus on customer retention and loyalty insights

## Why I Built This
I wanted to create a project that goes beyond basic dashboards and focuses on solving real business problems using data.
Instead of only visualizing sales, I explored questions around customer loyalty, repeat purchases, subscriptions, and spending behavior to understand how analytics can support customer retention and how can businesses grow.

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Repeat Customer Rate
- Subscription Conversion Rate
- Top Revenue Category

## Key Insights
- Young adult customers contributed the highest revenue
- Repeat buyers formed a major share of the customer base
- Certain products showed strong discount dependency
- Subscribers demonstrated stronger purchasing consistency
  
## Business Recommendations
- Increase discounts on low-performing categories
- Target subscription offers to repeat buyers
- Promote top-rated products during seasonal peaks
